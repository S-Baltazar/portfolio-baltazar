# Baltazar Salerno — Portfolio

Personal portfolio of **Baltazar Salerno** (Frontend Developer · Full Stack Developer · Software Engineer), built with **Next.js**, **TypeScript**, and **Tailwind CSS**.

🌐 **Live Application (Vercel):** [https://baltazarsalerno.vercel.app](https://baltazarsalerno.vercel.app)

---

## 🏆 Hackathon Awards & Recognition

Both **QWind** and **Tango Horizon** won **1st Place in the Impact Category** in their respective international hackathons:

- **1st Place (Impact Category) & 3rd Place Overall in Argentina — IonQ & Qollab Quantum Hackathon** (*QWind: Quantum Optimization for Wind Farms*, executed on real IonQ Forte 1 quantum hardware)
- **1st Place Local Winner (Impact Category) — NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026** (*Tango Horizon: 3D & AI Mars Mission Simulator*, Buenos Aires local event)
